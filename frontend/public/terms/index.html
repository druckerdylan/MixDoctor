1. What the service does

You upload an audio file. The site runs a fixed set of signal measurements over it — loudness, true peak, dynamic range, stereo and phase behaviour, frequency balance, masking, transients and related figures — compares those numbers against reference targets for a genre you choose, and returns a report of scores, findings and suggested corrective moves.

The output is numbers and words. It is never audio. The service does not process, master, alter, enhance or return your recording, and no version of your file is produced or delivered by it.

2. It is a measuring instrument, not an engineer
