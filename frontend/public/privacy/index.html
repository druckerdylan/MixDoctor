Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 August 2026

Your music is deleted as soon as it has been measured. It is never stored, never sent to a third party, and never used to train anything. That is the whole policy; the rest of this page is the detail behind it.

1. Audio you upload

When you analyze a mix, the file is streamed to a temporary location on the analysis server, read into memory, measured, and then deleted immediately as part of the same request. Deletion is unconditional: it happens if the analysis succeeds, if it fails with an error, and if the upload is rejected for exceeding the size limit.

Specifically, your audio is not:

  • written to any permanent store, bucket, archive or backup;
  • placed on a content-delivery network or given a shareable URL;
  • sent to any third party, including any AI provider;
  • used to train, fine-tune or evaluate any model;
  • listened to, retained for review, or kept after the response is returned.

Because nothing is retained, there is no stored copy for anyone to request, export or breach.

2. Reference tracks

An optional reference track is handled the same way and more narrowly still. It is read once and reduced to a small set of aggregate figures — overall loudness, stereo width, spectral balance and dynamics — which are compared against your mix to produce a difference. The reference is never separated into stems, never reproduced in listenable form, never returned to you, and is deleted with the rest of the request.

3. What is kept

Without an account

Nothing about your analysis is stored. The report exists in your browser for as long as the page is open. Closing or reloading the tab discards it.

With an account

If you choose to create an account, the site stores your e-mail address, a securely hashed password, and the results of analyses you run while signed in: file name, genre, score and findings. No audio is stored. You can ask for the account and all of its history to be deleted at any time by writing to the contact address above.

In your own browser

The list of plugins you own is saved in your browser's local storage so you do not have to enter it again. It stays on your device, is not an account, and is cleared when you clear site data.

4. Server logs

The hosting provider keeps ordinary operational logs — timestamp, request path, response status, IP address, user agent — for a short period, for security and debugging. These logs are a normal function of the host and do not contain your audio.

5. Analytics

The site is built to support Google Analytics 4, but only when a measurement ID has been configured. At the time of writing, no analytics is configured on mixdiagnostic.com: no analytics script is loaded, no analytics cookie is set, and no analytics request leaves your browser. If that changes, this page will be updated and the configuration will use IP anonymization.

6. The optional written summary

A written engineer's summary can be generated on top of the measurements by a third-party language model. This feature is currently disabled on mixdiagnostic.com. If it is ever enabled, what is sent is the numeric findings and their text descriptions — never your audio, and never anything that could be used to reconstruct it.

7. Cookies

No advertising or tracking cookies are set. If you sign in, a token is stored so the session persists. With analytics disabled there is no analytics cookie.

8. Donations

The tip jar links out to a third-party payment provider. Payments happen entirely on that provider's site under its own privacy policy. No card or payment details ever reach this site.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct, export or delete personal data held about you. Since no audio is retained and no account is required, the only personal data that can exist is an account record you created yourself. Write to the contact address above and it will be deleted.

10. Children

The service is not directed at children under 13, and accounts are not knowingly created for them.

11. Changes

This policy may be updated; the date at the top changes when it is. Material changes to how uploads are handled will be described here rather than made quietly.
