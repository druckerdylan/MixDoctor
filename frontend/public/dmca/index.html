2. Context that may answer your concern first

3. Filing a notice of claimed infringement

To be effective under 17 U.S.C. §512(c)(3), a written notice to the agent above must include all six of the following:

  1. A physical or electronic signature of the copyright owner, or a person authorised to act on their behalf.
  2. Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed.
  3. Identification of the material claimed to be infringing, with enough detail to locate it.
  4. Contact information for you: address, telephone number and e-mail address.
  5. A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the use is not authorised by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  6. A statement that the information in the notice is accurate, and — under penalty of perjury — that you are authorised to act on behalf of the owner.

Notices are reviewed and acted on promptly. Please note that under §512(f) a person who knowingly misrepresents that material is infringing may be liable for damages, including costs and legal fees.

Records of notices received and action taken are kept for the purpose of applying this policy consistently.
